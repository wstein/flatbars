A cross-cutting review of the whole ADR set ([001](/adr/adr-0001-structural-parser-and-walker/)–[013](/adr/adr-0013-labelled-loops/))
once they were drafted together. The goal was not to re-approve each record but to find the *seams
between* them — the places where two ADRs are individually true and jointly misleading. Same cast as
the ADR-001 review. Suggestions rated 1–5 (5 = adopt now).

:::note
*Post-review status (2026-06-01).* Several rating-5 items below were actioned in the same pass:
the two extension axes are now named in [adr-0004-token-lexer-and-grammar-seams](/adr/adr-0004-token-lexer-and-grammar-seams/);
superset-vs-isolation is sharpened in [adr-0003-strict-layering](/adr/adr-0003-strict-layering/)/[adr-0005-dialect-ladder](/adr/adr-0005-dialect-ladder/);
the engine-conformance checklist landed in [host-api](/engine/host-api/) §7.7; the "ClassicBars" term is split in
[adr-0008-rawbars](/adr/adr-0008-rawbars/)/[adr-0009-classicbars](/adr/adr-0009-classicbars/) and the RawBars docstring fixed; the
directives decision is recorded in [adr-0010-header-directives](/adr/adr-0010-header-directives/) and the `mustache` alias added
to [directives](/engine/directives/). *Still open (code, not docs):* the recursion guard
([adr-0012-recursion-budget](/adr/adr-0012-recursion-budget/)), the two regression tests, and the loop-variable warn-always
change.
:::

## The tensions found

### 1. Two different "extension points" wear one word

*Devi:* "ADR-004 warns that a third *grammar seam* would be a smell, then ADR-007 cheerfully adds new
*sigils*. A reader will think MinBars is the forbidden third seam. It isn't — there are *two
orthogonal axes* and we never named them. Axis A is the **grammar seam**: `parseExpr`/`parseHead`,
how a dialect parses a tag *interior* (`Parser.purs:57`). Axis B is the **shape gate**: `extras` and
the MinBars sigils, which *tag shapes* a dialect admits (`Parser.purs:271`). Adding sigils grows axis
B and is fine; adding a third grammar seam grows axis A and is the smell."

*Mara:* "Agreed, and this is the deeper point behind ADR-001's reword. The parser is *name*-agnostic
(axis-free), *shape-aware* and *shape-gated* (axis B), and *grammar-pluggable* (axis A). Three
sentences, three mechanisms. Right now ADR-004 blurs A and B in one 'Watch' note."

*Consensus:* name the two axes explicitly in ADR-004 and cross-link ADR-007's sigils to axis B.
*Rating 5.*

### 2. "RawBars ⊂ ClassicBars ⊂ MaxBars" vs. "no dialect imports another"

*Petra:* "ADR-003 says dialects never import each other; ADR-005 says `maxbars → classicbars` is fine.
Both are in the record, a click apart. That looks like the dogma cracked."

*Kai:* "It didn't crack — it was never about imports. `⊂` is a *surface-superset* claim: every MaxBars
template that avoids operators is a ClassicBars template. Package isolation is a *different* property,
and RawBars *does* have it (ClassicBars-free closure, ADR-003). MaxBars deliberately trades isolation for
reuse because it genuinely *is* a superset. The fix is wording: say 'no dialect imports another
*except along the ladder's superset edge*,' and state plainly that `⊂` ≠ isolation."

*Soren:* "Then make it falsifiable: a test asserting RawBars' closure excludes `classicbars`, so the one
allowed exception can't quietly become two."

*Consensus:* reword both ADRs to separate *superset* from *isolation*; add the closure test.
*Rating 5.*

### 3. The conformance checklist is promised in three ADRs and exists in none

*Soren:* "ADR-001 promised an engine-conformance checklist. ADR-002 leans on it for safety. ADR-007
needs it to even define what 'MinBars conforms' means. It is load-bearing in three records and
written in zero files. We are accumulating an IOU."

*Devi:* "It's small, though — escape fn, stringify, truthiness default, raw-output stance, sigil set,
real-AST-shape-or-none. That's a table in `host-api.adoc`, not a research project."

*Mara:* "Build it now, precisely because three ADRs depend on it. An undefined contract is worse than
no contract — it lets every engine claim conformance."

*Consensus:* write the checklist into [host-api](/engine/host-api/) before MinBars starts. *Rating 5.*

### 4. Does MinBars retro-justify or violate ADR-003's "extract lazily"?

*Mara:* "ADR-007 forces *new* kernel features — a per-engine truthiness default, a recursion guard.
ADR-003 said extract lazily. Is MinBars making us extract *eagerly* for a thing that isn't built?"

*Kai:* "The opposite. 'Extract lazily' means *on a proven second consumer*. The truthiness default
already has two consumers the moment MinBars needs `mustache` while the others need `handlebars` —
that *is* the second consumer. Same for the recursion guard: it hardens the reference `partialH`
*today* (`Prelude.purs:501` is unbounded) and serves MinBars tomorrow. These are textbook lazy
extractions, not speculation."

*Devi:* "One caveat — do them as *P0 shared prereqs* (ADR-007's own phase plan) and keep them green
under the existing suites, or we destabilise three shipped dialects for one unshipped one."

*Consensus:* ADR-007's P0 ordering is correct; note in ADR-003 that MinBars is the worked example of
its lazy-extraction rule. *Rating 4.*

### 5. The loop-variable lint only fires with a schema

*Soren:* "ADR-006 catches `length`/`first`/`key` shadowing *only when a schema is present*. Most
templates have no schema. That's the majority case going unguarded for a footgun we *introduced* by
dropping `@`."

*Kai:* "Without a schema we can't know `first` is a data field rather than the loop var — there's no
oracle. Erroring blindly would break legitimate templates."

*Soren:* "Not an error — a *warning*. 'bare `first` resolves to the loop variable here; wrap the field
as `(lookup this \"first\")` if you meant the datum.' Warn always, error only with a schema."

*Mara:* "That's the right severity split and it matches how `escapingWarnings` already behaves —
warn-by-default, never silently wrong."

*Consensus:* ADR-006 gains a schema-less *warning* tier under the schema-present *error*. *Rating 4.*

### 6. Granularity: are 003/004/005 over-split?

*Devi:* "Three ADRs for one migration quarter. Merge?"

*Petra:* "No — they have *different reversal costs*. Re-layering packages (003) is a quarter of work;
swapping a grammar seam (004) is a function type; rearranging the ladder (005) is a dependency edge.
A future change touches one, not all three. Separate records, heavily cross-linked, is correct."

*Consensus:* keep them separate. *Rating 4* (for the cross-linking, which was thin in the first
drafts).

### 7. "ClassicBars" names two different things (ADR-008/009)

*Kai:* "ADR-008 says RawBars' closure is *ClassicBars-free*, then RawBars' own docstring says it renders
'against the ClassicBars reference engine.' Both ship. A reader cannot tell whether RawBars uses ClassicBars
or not."

*Devi:* "Because 'ClassicBars' is overloaded. There's *ClassicBars the engine* — value policy + prelude +
`lower`, which moved to `kernel` (ADR-003) — and *ClassicBars the package/dialect*, which is now only the
surface desugar + renderers + catalog. RawBars uses the former (via `kernel`) and not the latter. The
prose conflates them, and ADR-008's own *Con* admits the docstring is stale."

*Mara:* "This is the same disease as tension #1 and #2 — one word, two referents. Decide the
vocabulary: 'the *reference engine*' = the `kernel` machinery; 'the *ClassicBars dialect*' = the package.
Then RawBars' sentence becomes true: 'renders against the reference engine, with no surface desugar.'"

*Petra:* "And fix the RawBars docstring in the same change, or we've documented the bug instead of
the decision."

*Consensus:* split the term — *reference engine* (kernel) vs *ClassicBars dialect* (package) — in ADR-008/009
and the RawBars docstring. *Rating 4.*

## Consensus summary

1. *Name the two extension axes* — grammar seam (`parseExpr`/`parseHead`) vs. shape gate
   (`extras`/sigils) — in ADR-004; stop letting one word cover both.
1. *Separate "superset" from "isolation"* in ADR-003/005; the ladder's `⊂` is about surface, the
   isolation rule is about imports, and they have exactly one sanctioned crossing (`maxbars → classicbars`).
   (Later narrowed: the 2026-06-04 ADR-005 amendment redefines `⊂` as surface-superset *modulo
   documented exceptions* — MaxBars borrows most of ClassicBars's surface, not all, so Kai's "genuinely
   *is* a superset" above holds only up to those exceptions.)
1. *Write the engine-conformance checklist now* — it is load-bearing in ADR-001/002/007 and exists
   nowhere.
1. *MinBars is the worked example of lazy extraction*, not a violation of it; its P0 prereqs
   (truthiness default, recursion guard) land first and stay green.
1. *The loop-variable shadow check warns always, errors with a schema.*
1. *003/004/005 stay separate* — different reversal costs — but cross-linked.
1. *Disambiguate "ClassicBars"* — the *reference engine* (the `kernel` machinery) vs the *ClassicBars
   dialect* (the package: surface desugar + renderers + catalog). RawBars uses the former, not the
   latter; the prose must say so.

## Potential future developments

* *[ADR-012](/adr/adr-0012-recursion-budget/) (recursion & resource budget)* — now drafted
  (Proposed); the depth/budget model (default 64, directive override, overflow behaviour) still needs
  *implementation*, and it touches MinBars, labelled loops, and self-including partials.
* *[ADR-013](/adr/adr-0013-labelled-loops/) (labelled loops)* — now drafted (Proposed); lifts the
  ADR-006 §5 v1 outer-loop limit once a naming/scoping design is settled.
* *Set-delimiters* shipped MinBars-only ([ADR-015](/adr/adr-0015-set-delimiters/) +
  2026-06-04 scope narrowing); the dialect ladder deliberately excludes them. A future
  cross-dialect demand reopens that ADR rather than the shape-gate axis.
* *A third grammar seam*, if one is ever proposed, triggers a review of axis A rather than a quiet
  addition (ADR-004 Watch).

## Concrete suggestions for improvement

1. *Land the four rating-5 doc fixes* (axes naming, superset≠isolation wording, the checklist, the
   cross-links) — they are corrections, and the set is quotably ambiguous without them.
1. *Add the engine-conformance checklist to [host-api](/engine/host-api/)*: escape fn · stringify · truthiness
   default · raw-output safety stance · admitted sigil set · real-AST shape (or "none").
1. *Add two regression tests*: (a) `parse` yields the same `Sep` shape for `else` and `user` (ADR-001's
   crown jewel); (b) RawBars' dependency closure excludes `classicbars` (ADR-003/005's isolation claim).
1. *Implement the recursion guard as ADR-007 P0* and immediately point the reference `partialH` at it,
   closing the unbounded-recursion gap that exists *today* regardless of MinBars.
1. *Promote the loop-variable shadow check to warn-always* with the schema-present error retained.
1. *Add an ADR index page* (this nav group) so the set is navigable and new ADRs have an obvious home.
1. *Fix the RawBars module docstring* to say "the reference engine" rather than "the ClassicBars reference
   engine," matching ADR-008/009's split term and ADR-008's own *Con*.
