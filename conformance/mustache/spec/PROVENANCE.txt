The FULL official suite — every module, including the optional ones marked `~`
(~dynamic-names, ~inheritance, ~lambdas). This is deliberate: the conformance
score MUST be measured against the whole spec so it cannot over-claim. Modules
MinBars does not (yet) implement show up as honest misses in the report.

Re-vendor (bump the SHA to match scripts/vendor-mustache.mjs):

  SHA=<sha>
  for m in comments delimiters interpolation inverted partials sections \
           '~dynamic-names' '~inheritance' '~lambdas'; do
    curl -s "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mustache/spec/$SHA/specs/$m.json" \
      -o "conformance/mustache/spec/$m.json"
  done
